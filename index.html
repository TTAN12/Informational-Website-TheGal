Our Mission

We aim to enhance the Philippine art industry by streamlining inventory management and accelerating the sale of backroom art pieces. Supporting the development of Filipino artists is at the heart of what we do.

Our Vision

To build an innovative art ecosystem that empowers galleries, collectors, and artists alike.

The Digital Gallery

Introducing The Digital Gallery

The Digital Gallery, a pioneering digital service redefining how art galleries and artists connect with audiences. Focused on accelerating the sales of backroom art pieces, The Digital Gallery empowers galleries with digital tools to overcome the challenges of limited exposure and sales inefficiencies. The Digital Gallery was originally conceptualized to be an inventory management system to address the analog needs of the galleries but through further validation with galleries, generating sales of backroom pieces which are pieces usually left unsold after an art exhibition turns out to be all local galleries’ most common hurdle. Our mission is to build an innovative art ecosystem that empowers galleries, collectors and artists and our Enhance the Philippine art industry by making backroom artwork accessible to more people through an accessible digital service. According to the most recent economic impact report from the National Commission for Culture and the Arts, total annual sales from both public and private art galleries surpassed 30 million USD in 2020 alone. This figure is projected to rise steadily as art continues gaining popularity among both domestic and international collectors.

How The Digital Gallery Stands Out

  • Exclusive Focus on Backroom Art of Galleries: Unlike Filipino Art, which features popular and current works, The Digital Gallery specializes in promoting and selling backroom pieces—hidden treasures that galleries struggle to showcase using traditional methods
  • Social media powered marketing: By leveraging the rising trends of social media integration, The Digital Gallery ensures that these backroom pieces reach the right audience, improving sales conversions—a feature competitors like Filipino Art and Drybrush do not offer

The Problem

  • Unseen Filipino Masterpieces: Limited space leaves culturally significant backroom artworks hidden.
  • Sales Inefficiencies: Manual sales methods limit scalability and profit potential.
  • High Inventory Costs: Storing unsold pieces burdens galleries financially.
  • Low Digital Inclusion: Many galleries lack the digital presence needed to reach modern buyers.
  • Emerging Artists’ Struggles: Filipino artists often lack the visibility needed to showcase their talent.

Target Users

B2B:

  • Galleries: Optimize sales of backroom pieces to reduce storage costs...
  • Independent Filipino Artists: Broaden their reach to new audiences and markets.

B2C:

  • Collectors & Investors: Discover and acquire unique Filipino artworks.
  • Interior Designers: Access curated collections for themed projects.
  • Emerging Enthusiasts: Engage with authentic Filipino art at accessible price points.

The Product

The Digital Gallery provides a transformative service for galleries to showcase, list and organize galleries’ backroom pieces on our platform for us to market, boost exposure and increase sales.

Core Features

  • Digital Marketplace: A seamless platform dedicated to selling backroom pieces from Filipino galleries and artists.
  • Customer-Driven Marketing: Targeted campaigns to connect Filipino art with its ideal audience.
  • Inventory Management: The platform will incorporate tools to streamline galleries' inventory tracking and organization, addressing operational inefficiencies.
  • Data Analytics (Subscription): Actionable insights for galleries to better understand buyer behavior and trends, providing data regarding prices on artworks and artists.

Value Proposition

  • For Galleries: Increased exposure and sales, particularly for Filipino backroom art.
  • For Artists: A platform to connect with both local and international buyers.
  • For Buyers: Easy access to unique Filipino art treasures, often unseen in traditional galleries.
