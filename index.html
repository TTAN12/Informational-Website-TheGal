Welcome to the Digital gallery

We are a digital service dedicated to assisting art galleries selling their backroom art pieces

Our Mission

We aim to enhance the Philippine art industry by streamlining inventory management and accelerating the sale of backroom art pieces. Supporting the development of Filipino artists is at the heart of what we do.

Our Vision

To build an innovative art ecosystem that empowers galleries, collectors, and artists alike.

Why LAS 120 Digital Gallery?

LAS 120 Digital Gallery offers efficient inventory management tools that simplify the way galleries track and manage their artwork. Our platform also accelerates sales by bringing hidden gems from backroom collections to the forefront, allowing them to reach a wider audience. Additionally, we provide vital support for Filipino artists by connecting emerging talent with potential buyers, helping them gain the exposure and recognition they deserve.

What We Offer

Our platform provides comprehensive solutions for the art industry. For galleries, we offer inventory management tools, data analytics to understand sales patterns and customer behavior, and a marketplace to showcase and sell backroom pieces. Collectors and buyers gain access to exclusive, high-quality artwork with a seamless browsing and purchasing experience. For artists, we provide increased visibility and direct connections to potential buyers, fostering growth and opportunities in their careers.
